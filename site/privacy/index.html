Privacy

The site stores what you type into the demo-request form and nothing else. The demo and the Windows app keep your pictures and video on your own device.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics and no advertising or tracking scripts.
  • Every script, font and model file is served from this site. Nothing is loaded from another company.
  • The site is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify sees the IP address each request comes from in order to answer it.

The demo-request form

The form asks for your name, work email, company, what you want to watch and an optional message. Netlify stores the submission for us.

  • We use it to reply to you about a demo, and for nothing else.
  • It is not sold, shared or added to a mailing list.
  • To have your request deleted, send a second one through the form with the same email address and say so in the message.

The demo in your browser

The demo downloads a model file to your browser and runs it there.

  • A sample frame, a photo you choose and frames from your camera are all processed on your device. None of them is uploaded.
  • Your browser asks before the page can use the camera. The camera turns off when you stop the demo or leave the page.
  • Nothing from the demo is saved, on your device or anywhere else.

The Windows app

The app makes no network connections. It has no account, no telemetry and no update check.

  • Video from a file or a webcam is processed on your PC.
  • It writes an event log and any screenshots you take to the logs folder beside the program, zones you draw to zones, and errors to sentinel.log.
  • Those files stay on your PC until you delete them.

People on camera

Sentinel Vision counts people and does not identify them. There is no face recognition and no names. Each person gets a number for the session, and the number is dropped when they leave the frame.

If you point it at other people, you are the one recording them. The responsible use section says what the statuses can and cannot tell you.
